# Product Strategy as of Week 6 - October 15

## User Group

Our primary users are students and working professionals aged 18–34 who are interested in fitness, exercise and being physically active but may struggle with limited time, consistency, knowledge in planning their workout, and consistency.

Statistics Canada (2025) reported that young adults aged 18–34 were the most physically active adult age group in Canada, with 65% of men and 53% of women meeting the recommended 150 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity per week.

Undergraduate students in Canada identified school obligations (68%) and time commitments (58%) as common barriers to physical activity (Pellerine et al., 2022) Similarly, working professionals were motivated to exercise to improve their fitness (40%) and health (26%), but lack of time (33%), fatigue from work (21%), and difficulty maintaining consistency (14%) prevents regular participation, and insufficient management support (Signorini et al., 2022; Safi et al. (2022).

These findings support our decision to focus on students and working professionals who want to start or maintain a fitness routine but need a more efficient and personalized approach.

## User Segments

**Primary segment: Fitness beginners**

Students and working professionals aged 18–35 who want to exercise but lack the knowledge, time, or confidence to create and follow an effective routine.

Main need: Guidance on what to do, when to do it, and how to get started

**Secondary segment: Experienced fitness users**

Individuals already familiar with exercising who want to maintain routines, track progress, and efficiently adjust workouts as their goals or circumstances change.

Main need: Efficient planning, flexibility, and measurable progress.

**Shared characteristic: Time-constrained users**

Individuals balancing academic, professional, and personal commitments who need realistic routines they can sustain at home, at the gym, or outdoors.

Main need: Fitness that adapts to their lives, rather than requiring their lives to revolve around fitness.

## Core Problems of Users

**Time constraints:** Users find it difficult to fit planning and physical activity into their schedules.

**Planning complexity:** Users may not know which exercises, intensity, frequency, or progression are appropriate for their goals.

**Difficulty maintaining consistency:** A missed workout or schedule change can disrupt an established routine.

**Limited progress visibility:** Users may struggle to evaluate their improvements or know when to adjust their plans.

**Lack of personalization:** Generic workout recommendations may not reflect available equipment, preferred activities, energy levels, or fitness experience.

# Customer Experience Vision

## Customer Experience Vision

Our fitness app aims to make fitness planning and maintaining an active lifestyle feel simple, manageable, and motivating. From creating their first workout plan to tracking their progress, users should feel confident and supported without spending too much time figuring out what to do. The experience should adapt to their everyday schedules and changing needs while keeping them in control of their fitness journey.

## 1.1 Key Moments

**Getting Started: Simple and Welcoming**  
Users can quickly create an account or continue as a guest. A short, guided onboarding process collects their fitness goals, experience, availability, workout preferences, and available equipment without overwhelming them with questions.  
Desired experience: "Getting started is easy, even if I don't know much about fitness routines."

**Creating a Personalized Workout Plan: Quick and Relevant**  
AI generates a weekly workout plan based on the user's goals, fitness level, schedule, and available resources. Users can review the plan, manually customize it, or ask AI to make adjustments.  
Desired experience: "I have a workout plan that fits my goals and lifestyle without spending too much time planning."

**Starting a Workout: Clear and Actionable**  
Users can easily see their scheduled activities, exercise instructions, duration, and progress. They can start a workout without having to make additional planning decisions.  
Desired experience: "I know what to do today and can get started right away."

**Daily Check-in: Adaptive and Supportive**  
A quick daily check-in allows users to share how they feel, including energy, sleep, soreness, and physical condition. fitness app can suggest adjustments, lighter activities, or recovery when appropriate, while leaving the final decision to the user.  
Desired experience: "My workout routine can adjust based on how I'm feeling today."

**Tracking Progress: Visible and Meaningful**  
After completing activities, users can record their workouts, review progress, and see how their efforts contribute to their fitness goals. Small achievements and consistent activity are recognized through encouraging feedback.  
Desired experience: "I can see my improvements and feel motivated to continue."

**Maintaining Consistency: Flexible and Motivating**  
When users miss a workout or their schedule changes, fitness app helps them adjust their routine without making them feel discouraged. A supportive avatar and optional rewards encourage engagement, including on recovery days.  
Desired experience: "Even if I miss a day, I can easily continue my fitness routine."

## 1.2 Interaction Principles

**Simplicity and Efficiency**  
Minimize the number of steps required to create, modify, and follow a fitness plan. Prioritize clear navigation, straightforward instructions, and quick access to essential features.

**Personalized and Adaptive**  
Provide workout recommendations that reflect users' goals, fitness levels, schedules, workout environments, and changing circumstances rather than relying on fixed routines.

**User Control and Flexibility**  
Use AI to assist with decision-making rather than replace it. Users should be able to review, modify, accept, or reject recommendations and customize their routines.

**Supportive, Not Pressuring**  
Use encouraging feedback, a friendly avatar, and optional rewards to build motivation without creating guilt around missed workouts, rest days, or broken streaks.

**Clear and Meaningful Progress**  
Present workout history, achievements, and progress toward personal goals in a way that is easy to understand and relevant to each user's fitness journey.

**Accessible and Inclusive**  
Support different fitness experience levels, time commitments, physical abilities, and workout environments, including home, gym, and outdoor activities

**Responsible and Transparent AI**  
Make AI recommendations understandable, respect users' privacy and data preferences, and communicate that fitness and nutrition guidance does not replace professional medical advice.

## Deliberate Trade-offs

**1. Simplicity Over Feature Complexity**  
Prioritize a clear, efficient workout planning experience instead of introducing too many features at launch.

**2. Core Fitness Planning Over Comprehensive Wellness Management**  
Focus first on workout planning, execution, and progress tracking rather than developing a complete nutrition, social, and wellness platform.

**3. Personalization Over Rigid Workout Plans**  
Allow workout recommendations to change with users' schedules and needs, even if this requires additional flexibility in the planning process.

**4. User Control Over Full AI Automation**  
AI will suggest and assist, but users will retain control over their workout decisions rather than automatically applying every recommendation.

**5. Sustainable Engagement Over Daily Exercise Pressure**  
Encourage consistency and progress without requiring daily workouts or penalizing users for rest days and missed sessions.

**6. Essential Information Over Extensive Onboarding**  
Collect only the information necessary to generate a useful initial plan. Additional preferences, health data, and nutrition information can be provided later.

**7. Practical Fitness Guidance Over Medical Expertise**  
Provide general workout and nutrition guidance without positioning the app as a replacement for medical professionals, registered dietitians, or certified fitness professionals.
